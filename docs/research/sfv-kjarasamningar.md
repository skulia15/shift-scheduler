# SFV collective agreements: working-time and shift-work rules for nursing-home staff

Researched 2026-11-09. Scope: private and non-profit nursing homes in SFV (Samtök fyrirtækja í velferðarþjónustu): Hrafnista, Grund/Mörk/Ás, Eir/Skjól/Hamrar, Sóltún, Vigdísarholt and others. This file confirms or corrects [icelandic-rules.md](icelandic-rules.md), which was built from state and Reykjavík texts because the SFV texts could not be opened then.

**Bottom line.** SFV agreements copy the public-sector shift-work chapter almost word for word:

- the 2019–2023 rounds carried betri vinnutími as *Fylgiskjal 2*;
- the 2024–2028 rounds moved it into the main text from **1 Nov 2024**.

The solver can use one rule set for Efling, Sameyki, SLFÍ and FÍH staff at SFV homes. The few differences between unions are listed below.

## Differences between unions (SFV homes)

- **Base text is the same.** Efling, Sameyki and FÍH at SFV all have:
  - 36 h;
  - vægi 1.05 / 1.2 with the 32 h floor;
  - vaktaálag 33.33 / 55 / 65 / 75 / 120 / 165%;
  - the same breytingargjald;
  - roster notice of 6 weeks (draft) and 1 month (final);
  - the 7.2 / 3.6 h holiday reduction;
  - 4–10 h shifts;
  - "að jafnaði" two consecutive days off.
- **Efling only:** the gr. 2.6.4 rule on short shifts (<8 h must be in the contract; ≥80% FTE get 8 h shifts first). It is not in F24 or S24. SLFÍ had a 2021 trial along the same lines (L21).
- **FÍH (nurses):** the 2024 text dropped the 55+ on-call exemption (nights only now) and the detailed 2-days-off rule (36 h split, no work on the rest day). Read literally, F24 is now *weaker* than F15/F20 on both. Bókun 4 keeps the 2021 stýrihópur guidance in force where it still applies.
- **Sameyki:** S24 2.6.11 keeps a legacy rule (50 min = 1 h for night and weekend duty) only for named staff employed on 1 Jan 1974. It is irrelevant today.
- **SLFÍ (sjúkraliðar):** the 2024 text was not read. The news item confirms 36 virkar stundir, the vaktahvati changes (3-month reference period) and the 1.62 h frítaka. These match F24/S24, so assume the same rules (**UNVERIFIED**).
- **Efling dispute:** Efling terminated the SFV agreement on 27 Feb 2025 over staffing standards. A mediator's proposal (Oct 2025) keeps the 2024 agreement in force to 31 Mar 2028, and Efling news (Nov 2025) says members approved changes. Their content was **not read**.

## Corrections to icelandic-rules.md

1. **SFV is now verified.** Its caveat "SFV … UNVERIFIED" can be lifted for Efling, Sameyki and FÍH: the values match.
2. **Stórhátíðardagar are 8 in SFV, not 9.** 1. maí is a sérstakur frídagur only (grandfathered for staff from before 2000). The list in icelandic-rules.md came from Reykjavík.
