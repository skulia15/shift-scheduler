### Worked example for the solver

Credited hours = Σ (clock hours × vægi). The target is 36 h/week × FTE, but the credited total may not let actual hours fall below 32 h/week × FTE (averaged over the pay period).

Example: a full-timer doing only weekday nights 00–08 gets 1.2 × clock hours credited, so 36 / 1.2 = 30 h of clock time would fill the target. The 32 h floor applies instead, so they work 32 clock hours.

## Public holidays (SLFÍ-RVK 2.1.4)

- **Sérstakir frídagar (17):** nýársdagur, skírdagur, föstudagurinn langi, laugardagur fyrir páska, páskadagur, annar í páskum, sumardagurinn fyrsti, 1. maí, uppstigningardagur, hvítasunnudagur, annar í hvítasunnu, 17. júní, frídagur verslunarmanna, aðfangadagur from 12:00, jóladagur, annar í jólum, gamlársdagur from 12:00.
- **Stórhátíðardagar (9, a subset):** nýársdagur, föstudagurinn langi, páskadagur, hvítasunnudagur, 1. maí, 17. júní, aðfangadagur from 12:00, jóladagur, gamlársdagur from 12:00.
- **Almennir frídagar:** Saturdays and Sundays.
- **Pay categories:**
  - special holidays are paid like weekends (55% / 75%);
  - stórhátíðardagar are paid at 120%;
  - Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve evenings (16–24) and the following nights (00–08) are paid at 165%.

## Typical nursing-home shift times (UNVERIFIED)

No primary source found. These are commonly used patterns in Icelandic healthcare and should be treated as configurable defaults:

- **morgunvakt** about 08:00–16:00 (sometimes 07:30/07:45 start);
- **kvöldvakt** about 15:30/16:00–23:30/24:00;
- **næturvakt** about 23:30/24:00–08:00;
- many homes also use short shifts (e.g. 08–12, 17–21).

A 2016 Hrafnista strike-exemption notice in Stjórnartíðindi confirms the three-shift split (dag / kvöld / nótt) with 8 h day and evening shifts. It gives no clock times.

## Encoding hints

- **Hard constraints:**
  - rest ≥ 11 h between shifts, or ≥ 8 h only at a scheduled shift-type change (morning→night etc.);
  - ≤ 13 h work per 24 h from the start of the working day;
  - ≥ 35 h weekly rest per 7 days (or 2 consecutive days per 14);
  - 48 h/week average over 6 months;
  - shift length 4–10 h unless locally agreed;
  - 55+ night opt-out;
  - credited hours ≥ 32 h × FTE.
- **Soft constraints:**
  - 8 h rest at shift change no more than once a week;
  - 2 consecutive days off per week (each ≥ 36 h if split);
  - fair holiday distribution;
  - weekend frequency;
  - overtime minimised;
  - 8 h average per 24 h for night workers.
