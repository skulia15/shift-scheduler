# Public-sector kjarasamningar 2024–2028: working-time rules for care-home shift staff

Researched 2026-11-09. Scope: municipal (Samband íslenskra sveitarfélaga, SNS), Reykjavíkurborg (RVK) and state (ríkið, FJR/SNR) agreements covering nurses (FÍH), sjúkraliðar (SLFÍ) and care floor staff (Sameyki, SGS, Efling). SFV agreements are out of scope here and are covered separately.

**Headline:** the 2024 round (signed June–July 2024) abolished the betri vinnutími annexes (Fylgiskjal 1/2/3) on **31 Oct 2024** and wrote their content into the main agreement text from **1 Nov 2024**. Shift-work rules are now in each agreement's chapter 2 (state and SNS: gr. 2.6; RVK–FÍH: gr. 2.5). The rules are nearly identical across employers, with a few real differences listed in [Differences that matter](#differences-that-matter).

## 11. Worked example: full-timer, 4-week roster over Christmas 2026

Roster period: **Mon 14 Dec 2026 – Sun 10 Jan 2027** (4 weeks, 28 days). Agreement: state or SNS text (same numbers). FTE 100%.

**Step 1: base duty.** 36 h × 4 = **144.0 h**.

**Step 2: holiday reduction (2.6.7).** Count sérstakir frídagar and stórhátíðardagar falling Mon–Fri in the period:

| Date | Day | Holiday | Reduction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 24 Dec 2026 | Thu | aðfangadagur (from 12:00) | 3.6 |
| 25 Dec 2026 | Fri | jóladagur | 7.2 |
| 26 Dec 2026 | Sat | annar í jólum (falls on a weekend) | 0 |
| 31 Dec 2026 | Thu | gamlársdagur (from 12:00) | 3.6 |
| 1 Jan 2027 | Fri | nýársdagur | 7.2 |
| **Total** | | | **21.6** |

Required vinnuskil = 144.0 − 21.6 = **122.4 credited hours**. The reduction applies whether or not those days are worked.

**Step 3: build the roster and credit each hour by band.** Shift types: D 08–16, E 16–24, N 00–08 (dated by the calendar day the shift is on).

| Date | Shift | Bands | Clock h | Credited |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mon 14 Dec | D | 8 × dagvinna (1.0) | 8 | 8.00 |
| Tue 15 Dec | E | 1 × dagvinna (16–17) + 7 × 33.33% (1.05) | 8 | 8.35 |
| Wed 16 Dec | E | same | 8 | 8.35 |
| Sat 19 Dec | D | 8 × 55% (1.05) | 8 | 8.40 |
| Mon 21 Dec | D | 8 × 1.0 | 8 | 8.00 |
| Thu 24 Dec | E | 8 × **165%** (1.2) | 8 | 9.60 |
| Sat 26 Dec | D | 8 × 55% (sérstakur frídagur, 1.05) | 8 | 8.40 |
| Mon 28 Dec | E | 1 × 1.0 + 7 × 1.05 | 8 | 8.35 |
| Tue 29 Dec | E | same | 8 | 8.35 |
| Thu 31 Dec | E | 8 × **165%** (1.2) | 8 | 9.60 |
| Mon 4 Jan | N | 8 × 75% (Mon night, 1.2) | 8 | 9.60 |
| Tue 5 Jan | N | 8 × 65% (1.2) | 8 | 9.60 |
| Wed 6 Jan | N | 8 × 65% (1.2) | 8 | 9.60 |
| Sat 9 Jan | D | 8 × 55% (1.05) | 8 | 8.40 |
| **Total** | 14 shifts | | **112** | **122.60** |

Credited 122.6 ≥ 122.4 required, so the employee is **+0.2 h** (12 min) over. That is within any sensible tolerance; otherwise it carries forward or is paid as overtime.

Checks:
- Each week has 2 consecutive days off: Thu–Fri 17–18 Dec, Tue–Wed 22–23 Dec, Fri–Sun 1–3 Jan, Thu–Fri 7–8 Jan.
- Rest ≥ 11 h everywhere; E→N happens only after 3 days off.
- 13 h/24 h is never exceeded.
- 48 h average: trivially met.

**Step 4: 32 h floor.** The vægi bonus is 122.6 − 112 = 10.6 h. How the 32 h floor combines with the holiday reduction is **not stated** in the texts.
- Read literally, the floor is 32 × 4 = 128 clock hours. That would cancel most of the holiday reduction, which contradicts 2.6.7, so it is unlikely.
- Proportional reading: floor = 122.4 × 32/36 = **108.8** clock hours.
- Subtractive reading: the vægi bonus is capped at 4 h/week × FTE, so floor = 122.4 − 16 = **106.4** clock hours.

At 112 clock hours this roster passes both plausible readings. **UNVERIFIED**: confirm with Vinnustund (state) or the SNS/RVK payroll rules before encoding.

**Part-timer at 80%:** required = 0.8 × 122.4 = **97.92** credited hours. The floor scales the same way (0.8 × the figure chosen above).

**Edge cases for the solver:**
- A day shift on aðfangadagur (Thu 08–16) splits: 08–12 is dagvinna (1.0), 12–16 is stórhátíð 120% (1.05). Credited 4 + 4.2 = 8.2.
- A night shift starting 23:30 on 24 Dec runs 23:30–24:00 at 165% and 00–08 on jóladagur at 165%, all at vægi 1.2.
- Banding must be done per minute against the calendar date of each minute, not the shift's start date.

## Corrections to icelandic-rules.md

1. **Sources and clauses:** the betri vinnutími content is no longer in "Fylgiskjal 2" (SLFÍ-RIKI 2020). Since 1.11.2024 it is in the main text: state and SNS gr. 2.6.6–2.6.9, FÍH-RVK gr. 2.5.6–2.5.9. Cite SLFÍ-RÍKI 2024 rather than the 2020 samkomulag.
2. **Row 3b (2 consecutive days off, 36 h split, union consent):** this is **RVK wording only**. State and SNS agreements say just "að jafnaði tvo samfellda frídaga í viku", which is soft and has no 36 h rule. The state wording is unchanged since at least 2019–2023.
3. **Row 4 (shift length 4–10 h):** **SGS–SNS allows 4–12 h**.
4. **Row 7d (55+ opt-out):** the SNS agreements (SGS, SAL) use **50+**. Only FÍH-RVK includes bakvaktir; state SLFÍ covers nights only.
5. **Public holidays:** the stórhátíðardagar count is **8 in the state and FÍH-RVK texts** (1. maí is a sérstakur frídagur only). It is **9 including 1. maí in SNS** (and in SLFÍ-RVK 2020). This should be a per-agreement setting.
6. **Row 1d / vægi table:** confirmed for the current texts. The 120%/165% bands date from the **2023** agreements; the 2020 texts had 90%/120%.
7. **Row 1b wording:** the 1.05 weight is defined by pay band (33.33%/55%). That includes Fri 17–24 and weekend or sérstakur frídagur 08–24, and also 120% stórhátíð 08–24.
8. **Row 6c:** add the **1.3% short-notice aukavakt fee** and the requirement for the employee's consent to any employer-driven change. The fee is the same whatever the FTE.
9. **Breaks:** the file is silent on breaks. Since 1.11.2024 eating on shift is working time, and the 25-min per-shift payment has been abolished.
10. **Row 5:** add LAW 57. gr. a (L. 58/2023), the working-time recording duty. Also note RAMMI-97 treats leave and sickness as "hlutlaus" (neutral) in averaging, which gives the same outcome as the statute.
11. **New:** the ≥ 20% definition of a shift worker, vaktahvati thresholds, the part-timer overtime rule (2.3.4–2.3.6), and frítökuréttur details (8 h planned rest earns none; ≥ 4 h blocks; never expires). Everything else in icelandic-rules.md is confirmed against the 2024 texts: 11 h, 13 h, 8 h at shift change at most about once a week, 35 h weekly rest, 2 days per 14, 48 h over 6 months, 6-week draft and 1-month final roster, 7.2/3.6 reduction, 32 h floor, vaktaálag table.
